I am from Madurai, one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, with a history of over 2,500 years. 
It is often called the Temple City and is world-famous for the Meenakshi Amman Temple, an architectural and cultural landmark. 
Madurai is considered the Cultural Capital of Tamil Nadu, known for its rich Tamil traditions, literature, and classical arts. 
Historically, it was the capital of the Pandya dynasty and served as an important trade center. 
Today, the city is also an educational hub, with institutions like Madurai Kamaraj University. 
The economy is diverse, covering textiles, IT, automobile, and agriculture. 
Madurai also attracts many tourists with places such as Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal, Gandhi Museum, Alagar Kovil, and Vaigai Dam. 
The city is equally popular for its unique cuisine, including Jigarthanda, Kari Dosa, and Parotta. Well connected by road, rail, and an international airport, Madurai is fondly nicknamed the “Athens of the East” for its architectural beauty.
